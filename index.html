08 / A few answers

Before we build.

Can you build my store from beginning to end?

Yes. Store setup, design, products and collections, navigation, payment and shipping configuration, testing and launch can all be included. We agree the exact scope first.

Do you work with Shopify and WooCommerce?

Yes. I build Shopify stores and WordPress websites with WooCommerce. I’ll help you choose the platform that suits your business and the way you want to manage it.

Do you only handle migrations?

No. Complete store builds and improvements are core services. Migrations are a specialist service for brands ready to move their existing store.

Will a migration affect my SEO?

I map agreed legacy URLs and test redirects to protect your SEO foundations. Rankings and traffic may fluctuate, so unchanged rankings are not guaranteed.

What happens after launch?

You receive a practical handover and defined post-launch support. Migrations include 14 days of support; the details for your build or sprint are agreed in the proposal.

How do we get started?

Send your current store link, product count and goals, or use the project scope builder. I’ll review the brief and reply with a clear next step.
