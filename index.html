The 887-Product Migration Rescue

Moving a high-volume catalogue from WooCommerce to Shopify is not just exporting a CSV. Here is how I safely replatformed 887 health, beauty & lifestyle SKUs for anchor brand Swiftglamour with zero traffic loss.

Visit Live Store ↗

The Problem

Database Overload & Complex Variants

887 products with multiple sizes and shade variations were timing out standard Shopify import apps and causing mobile checkout lag.

The Risk

Losing Organic Google Search Rankings

Without comprehensive 301 URL redirects, existing organic search traffic would hit broken 404 pages, destroying customer trust and sales.

The Solution

Custom Python Extraction & 1:1 Image Framing

Wrote custom ETL scripts to extract and clean product data, automated PhotoRoom to standardize 900+ images into 1:1 squares, and created a complete 301 redirect map.

The Result

Flawless Replatform · Zero Downtime

Store launched smoothly on a clean Shopify Liquid theme with fast 2-step mobile checkout, preserved SEO rankings, and happy buyers.
