Lessons That Followed
On Technology

I used to think Python was for data scientists. Now I use it to migrate thousands of products. The tool doesn't care who you are — it cares if you're willing to learn.

On Pricing

The amount I got for an 887-product migration was below market. The experience was the other payment. Next time the number goes up.

On Upwork

Every connect is limited. Spend strategically. My first win came with zero reviews. It's possible.

On Starting

I started on a 2GB phone. The laptop removed one obstacle. Hunger is the constant.

On Your Presence

I rented out my LinkedIn for cash. I can't fully access it now. Your online presence is not for sale.

On Middlemen

When you have the skills, find a way to own the client relationship directly. Middlemen always eat into your earnings.
